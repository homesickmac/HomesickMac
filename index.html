A Life Spent Playing the Blues

I've been fortunate to live my music dream for many years now. Playing the guitar and singing great stories from the early days of blues, hillbilly, country and other traditional styles has taught me a great deal about the world — and about myself.

Born in former Yugoslavia, I found my musical home in Scandinavia in 1992, and have spent the decades since performing, teaching and sharing the love for real, honest guitar music.

Nowadays I especially enjoy helping players of all ages find their way back to the guitar — or deeper into it — through my retreats, workshops, and one-on-one sessions.
