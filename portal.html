Welcome to the island.

Step by step

Getting to Ven

Before you travel: buy your ferry ticket in advance at ventrafiken.se. The crossing is short and scenic — about 25 minutes.
1

Head to Landskrona — Skeppskajen

The ferry terminal is at Skeppskajen in Landskrona. Follow signs to the harbour. There is parking nearby if you're driving.

2

Take the ferry to Ven

Tickets at ventrafiken.se. Buy in advance if you can — it's easier than queuing. The crossing takes around 25 minutes and the views are lovely.

3

Step off the ferry — the bus is waiting

Bus departures are synced with ferry arrivals, so there's no waiting around. Look for the bus with the sign Backafallsbyn.

4

Pay for the bus on board

Debit or credit card accepted on the bus. Alternatively, use the Skånetrafiken app if you already have it. Either way works fine.

5

Arrive at Backafallsbyn

You're here. Take a breath. The island is quiet and beautiful — you've made it.

Day 1

How Arrival Day works

🕛 Smooth start from 12:00

The Retreat officially begins at 15:00 — but feel free to arrive from noon. Drop your instrument in the Retreat Room, say hello to whoever's already there, warm up, ask Mac a question about a song. No pressure. The afternoon is yours to settle in at your own pace.

Lunch on Day 1 is at own cost. The venue restaurant is right there if you're hungry.

🏠 Bungalows

If not already by the time you arrive, the bungalows are ready after 15:00 — when we're already into the first session. You can drop your bags during the fika break at 16:30, or after we finish the first session at 18:00. Either works perfectly.

If you arrive early and need somewhere to leave luggage, just ask at the reception to open the Retreat Room for you — I'm there from 11:00.

🌙 First evening

After the first Retreat session wraps at 18:00, we have some free time before dinner at 19:00. The first evening meal is at the Backafallsbyn restaurant — we always go together, and it's a lovely way to kick off the Retreat. Dinner is at our own cost this evening.

After dinner and around 20:00: short technique demos, Q&A, then I'll play a short solo concert for you only (accompanying people are also welcome) — and if the porch calls — an evening jam in the bungalows.
