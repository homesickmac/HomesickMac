What Is the Retreat?

More Than Guitar Lessons.
A Community.

Since 2012, players from all walks of life have been gathering on the Island of Ven in southern Sweden for the three days on my Guitar Retreat that feel unlike anything else in a musician's year. I teach through songs — real songs with grooves and stories — not only licks in isolation, although we do work with those at times, when participants ask for it.

With over 85% of participants returning year after year, the Retreat has become something rare: a genuine community. People come for the guitar, but they keep coming back for each other.

All skill levels are welcome. Whether you played seriously in your youth and are returning after a long hiatus, or you've always wanted to explore the blues and other acoustic styles (including slide guitar) — you belong here.

What the Retreat Creates

A Friday Jam That Refuses to Stop

Johnny Petersen and Steve Howard from Malmö, who came to the Retreat together, had already been meeting on Fridays to play guitar together. During a Retreat, they mentioned their weekly sessions to a fellow participant Arne, who had just moved to that area and didn't know anybody he could maybe play with. They invited him to join, and he did.

The year after, a participant named Peter joined. Then Håkan. Then, through a later retreat, a participant Gert found his way into the group. Most recently, a participant Olle joined after moving back to the area.

They now have what they call a "hiring freeze" — when everyone shows up at once, there's barely room to fit all the guitars in.

The group has been meeting every Friday for over 10 years. They play by the same principles I teach at the Retreat: keep the volume considerate, encourage each other to play solid backup or a solo, choose songs that work for everyone.

A community that Retreat inspired, and there are no signs anybody's quitting anytime soon.

What Happens Between Retreats

The Community Keeps Playing

The summer Retreat at the Island of Ven ends after three days — but something it starts doesn't...

❝

A few of us who gather each year for the retreat on Ven agree on one thing: waiting an entire year in between is simply too long.

So, three of us from Gothenburg and three from Karlstad have started arranging mini-retreats — usually sometime in the autumn, and ideally once in the spring too. The format is much like Ven (just without Mac, of course). We arrive on Friday, cook together, share a few beers, and play for as long as we can keep going.

Besides these get-togethers, we Gothenburg folks have also formed a trio that meets every week to play whatever we feel like. We don't have a rehearsal room; instead, we take turns hosting at each other's homes.

Needless to say, all guitars are acoustic. Mac is really the spark behind all of this, and Ven will always be our number one. Still, these little initiatives make the wait a bit easier.

— Nisse Mällroth, Guitar Retreat Participant · Gothenburg, Sweden
