About Homesick Mac

Performer, teacher and eternal student of the blues.

Who Is Mac?

A Life Spent Playing the Blues

I've been fortunate to live my music dream for many years now. Playing the guitar and singing great stories from the early days of blues, hillbilly, country and other traditional styles has taught me a great deal about the world — and about myself.

Born in former Yugoslavia, I found music early and never let it go. After years of gigging and growing through Germany, I settled in Sweden in 1992 and have been performing across Scandinavia ever since — at blues festivals, guitar trade shows, intimate venues, and my very own Guitar Retreat on the Island of Ven.

I started performing professionally at 23. That's now more than 43 years ago — and the guitar still surprises me every week.

Teaching

"Who Am I to Teach Somebody Else…?"

That doubt has been spinning in my head since I was first asked to teach — at age 20. I still think about it. It keeps me honest.

I use my 45 years of playing experience to help guitarists reach their best potential. It's never about who is "better" — it's about where we each are on our music journey. I might be a few years ahead on the blues and slide path, so I can help someone avoid some of the hurdles I stumbled over myself.

My approach is song-first. A lick is meaningless if it doesn't serve a song or a groove. I find the fastest way to real progress for each player — and I especially love working with people who played seriously in their youth and are finding their way back to the instrument.
