Dates & Availability

Arrival from 12:00 noon on Monday.

The Retreat takes place at Backafallsbyn bungalow village on the Island of Ven, southern Sweden.
Sessions are held in a dedicated room with air conditioning. Evening jams happen in the bungalows.

Meals & Food

Included in all accommodation options: Breakfast and lunch on Day 2 and Day 3, plus the Swedish fika (coffee, tea, cakes and fruit) every day.

Arrival day (Day 1): most people arrive right around lunchtime, but that first lunch isn't included — it's at your own cost. The venue restaurant is right there if you're hungry when you arrive.

Evening meals are enjoyed together but are not included in the accommodation price — these are at your own cost.

Cancellation Policy

This applies to the tuition fee paid to Homesick Mac — accommodation cancellation terms are set by Backafallsbyn directly.

Until May 18th, 2026 — free cancellation, full refund if already paid.

May 18th – June 18th, 2026 — 50% of the tuition fee will be invoiced.

Within 30 days of the Retreat — the full tuition fee applies.
